NanoClaw

A private, self-hosted personal AI assistant for one household. It is not a public product and has no sign-up.

What it does

NanoClaw runs on a server owned by its operator, Itamar Lev. Family members chat with it through messaging apps, and it helps with everyday tasks by connecting to the Google account of the person it works for:

  • reading, searching, and drafting email in Gmail, and sending or organising it when asked;
  • viewing and managing events in Google Calendar;
  • finding and working with files in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides;
  • looking up Google Contacts, and working with Google Forms, Chat, and Photos when asked.

Who can use it

Only the operator and the family members he has set up. Anyone who connects a Google account does so themselves, and can remove NanoClaw's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
