NanoClaw privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who this applies to

NanoClaw is a private, self-hosted personal AI assistant that runs on a server owned by its operator, Itamar Lev, for his own household. It is not offered to the public. This policy describes how it handles the Google account data of the people who connect their own Google account to it.

What NanoClaw can access

When a person connects their Google account, NanoClaw asks for access to the following. It uses this access to carry out tasks that person has asked for, including recurring tasks they have set up (for example, watching for new email or preparing a daily briefing).

  • Gmail: read and search email; send email; and organise email (labels, archive, trash).
  • Google Calendar: view and edit calendar events.
  • Google Drive, Docs, and Slides: read files in Drive, and create or edit files that NanoClaw itself creates or opens.
  • Google Sheets: read and edit spreadsheets.
  • Google Contacts: read and edit contacts, and read other contacts and the directory.
  • Google Forms: view and edit forms.
  • Google Chat: read and send messages, and view and manage spaces and memberships.
  • Google Photos: view, upload, and organise items in the photo library.
  • Basic profile: your email address and name, to identify the connected account.

Where the data is stored

  • The Google sign-in credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored encrypted in a credential gateway that the operator runs on his own server. The assistant itself does not see them.
  • When the assistant works on a request, it may keep notes, summaries, and extracts from your Google data in its private workspace on that same server so that it can continue the work later.

How the data is used and shared

  • Google data is used only to carry out the tasks of the person it belongs to, and to provide NanoClaw's user-facing features to that person.
  • To produce its responses, NanoClaw sends the relevant content to the AI model provider it uses, Anthropic (Claude), through Anthropic's API. See Anthropic's commercial terms and privacy policy.
  • Google data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone else except as described above or where the law requires it.
  • Google data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

NanoClaw's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can remove NanoClaw's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask about, or request deletion of, anything the assistant has stored from your Google data, contact the operator below.
